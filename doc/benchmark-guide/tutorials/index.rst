Tutorials
=========

A tutorial is a lesson. You do the steps, and you get a working result.

The six tutorials tell one story: **you join the latency study.** You set
up a lab space of your own on a host with a NUCLEO-G474RE, and you measure
the IRQ entry latency of Zephyr. Then you ask a question of your own,
audit a result, and give your runs to a second lab space. Each tutorial
starts where the one before it stops. Together they take about one hour,
most of it board time.

The console output in the tutorials is real: it comes from a run on the
host cos-workstation on 2026-10-28. Your numbers, folder names, ids and
times are different. The numbers are examples of what the tools print,
not results of the study.
