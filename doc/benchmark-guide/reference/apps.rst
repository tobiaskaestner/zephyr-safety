The apps and their variants
===========================

Each :term:`app` has its documentation beside its code, in
``tests/benchmarks/<app>/doc/``, as the samples of Zephyr do. The guide
collects these pages: each page tells what the app measures, and gives
its options, its :term:`variants <Variant>` and its output.
:doc:`../explanation/apps` gives the frame that all measurement apps
share. :doc:`../howto/add-an-app` tells how to write the page of a new
app.
